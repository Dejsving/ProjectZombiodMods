Alembic Distilling adds portable and stationary distillation apparatuses for
Project Zomboid Build 42.

Assemble the alembic from a vanilla kettle, rubber hose, metal pipe and funnel.
Fill it with one supported alcoholic fluid, heat it in a compatible furnace or
fire, then use the "Distill in Portable Alembic" recipe. The calculation uses
the actual fluid amount and alcohol strength. Efficiency grows with Cooking
from 70 to 80 percent, and the recipe awards 2 Cooking XP per liter.

With MetalWelding level 4, a stationary still can be crafted from a bucket,
four small copper sheets and two metal pipes. It has a 10 litre working tank,
uses the vanilla stove fuel and fire-lighting interface, heats the room like a
fireplace, and transfers the resulting vodka to a selected container. Crafting
creates a heavy moveable item; place it in the world through the normal
moveable placement menu. The
firebox must be lit and contain at least 30 units of fuel before a batch starts.
